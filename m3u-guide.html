CREDENTIAL & PLAYLIST GUIDE

M3U and Xtream, explained simply.

Learn what each access format contains, what information you need and how to protect your credentials during setup.

What is an M3U link?

An M3U URL is a playlist address supplied by your authorized service provider. A compatible player reads that address to organize the content your account is permitted to access.

Treat the complete URL like a password. Do not publish it, paste it into unknown websites or share screenshots containing it.

What are Xtream-style credentials?

This format normally separates access into three fields: a server address, a username and a password. Your provider must supply the exact values and confirm which apps and devices are supported.

  1. Install a compatible player from a trusted app store.
  2. Choose the provider-login or Xtream-style option.
  3. Enter the server address, username and password exactly.
  4. Save, refresh the content and test playback.

Before asking for conversion help

  • Confirm that the account and content are legally authorized.
  • Send the device model and player name—not your password in a public chat.
  • Explain which format you received and which format your trusted player accepts.
  • Ask Support for provider-approved instructions.

Common setup problems

Credentials rejected

Check extra spaces, letter case, subscription status and the complete server address.

Playlist loads but playback does not

Test the network, restart the app and device, then ask whether your connection or region requires verification.

Only one device works

Connection limits depend on the selected service. Confirm the account rules before using another screen.

KEEP CREDENTIALS PRIVATE

Support does not need your password in a public post. Use the private contact channel and share only what is necessary.

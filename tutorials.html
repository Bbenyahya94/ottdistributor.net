BEGINNER DICTIONARY

Four terms to understand first.

These short definitions make subscriptions and reseller offers easier to compare.

SERVER / PANEL

The service option you choose. Each has its own positioning, availability and reseller rules.

CONNECTION

How many simultaneous devices or streams are allowed. Confirm the exact rule before ordering.

SUBSCRIPTION

Customer access for a fixed duration, commonly 1, 3, 6 or 12 months.

RESELLER CREDITS

A business balance used to create customer access. Credit conversion differs by server.

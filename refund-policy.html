COMMERCIAL POLICY

Refund policy.

This page explains how refund requests are reviewed. The written terms confirmed for your specific order take priority.

LAST UPDATED: AUGUST 2026ORDER-SPECIFIC REVIEW

Before payment

Ask Sales to confirm the exact service, duration, connection limit, trial status, device requirements, activation conditions and refund eligibility in writing. Product names alone do not guarantee a particular feature or quality level.

When a request may be reviewed

A refund request may be considered when the delivered access materially differs from the written order confirmation or when activation could not be completed for a reason attributable to the supplied service. Approval is not automatic and depends on the verified circumstances and applicable law.

Common exclusions

  • Incorrect device, app or network information supplied by the customer.
  • Change of mind after access has been delivered or used.
  • Credential sharing, prohibited use or violation of connection limits.
  • Temporary issues outside the provider's reasonable control.
  • Features or content that were never confirmed in writing.

How to submit a request

Email support@8kstream.net with your name, order reference, service, activation date, device, a clear description and evidence that hides all passwords. Support will acknowledge the request and explain the next review step.

Nothing on this page limits mandatory consumer rights that apply in your jurisdiction.

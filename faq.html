Practical information for IPTV resellers evaluating panels, credits and vendor support.

What is an IPTV reseller panel?

A backend workspace used to create trials, activate customer subscriptions, manage expiry dates and control reseller credits.

How do wholesale credits work?

The vendor defines how credits map to subscription duration and connections. Confirm the exact conversion before purchasing.

Can I test a panel first?

Functional trials may be available. Test the devices, regions and workflows relevant to your customers.

Do you support M3U and EPG?

Support differs by vendor panel. Confirm playlist format, EPG refresh behavior and credential persistence.

Can I create sub-resellers?

Eligible panels support delegated reseller accounts. Ask sales which vendors include multi-tier access.

Do I need my own server?

Not for cloud-hosted vendor panels. Self-hosted systems require infrastructure, security and maintenance.

Which devices can customers use?

Common profiles include Smart TV, Android TV, Fire TV, mobile, tablet and desktop. The exact player and setup method must be confirmed for the selected panel.

How fast is account delivery?

Delivery begins after payment and order verification. Timing depends on vendor availability; never promise instant delivery unless your current workflow supports it.

What subscription durations are available?

The catalog uses 1, 3, 6 and 12-month structures. Connection count and renewal rules can differ by panel.

Can a customer change panels later?

Usually this requires new credentials. Confirm unused time, migration and cancellation terms before changing service.

What information should a customer send?

Target panel, duration, connection count, country, main device and whether a trial is required. Never request unnecessary passwords or sensitive data.

Are availability and quality guaranteed?

No unverified guarantee should be published. Availability, resolution and service behavior must be confirmed through current vendor information and functional testing.

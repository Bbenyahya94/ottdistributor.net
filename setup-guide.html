OPERATOR GUIDE · 15 MIN

Launch your reseller operation step by step

A professional launch begins with verification and controlled testing—not bulk purchasing. Use this workflow for any panel in the directory.

COMPLIANCE FIRST: Confirm that you and the provider have authorization for the content, markets and customer use involved. Do not market unverified channel counts, uptime or licensing claims.

Define your customer profile

Write down target countries, languages, common devices, expected connections, resolution needs and realistic first-month volume.

Shortlist two or three panels

Use the directory and buyer guide to compare operational fit. Avoid choosing exclusively from branding or an advertised channel count.

Request official trial access

Test login creation, playlist or app credentials, EPG behavior, support response and stability during the periods your customers will use it.

Run a device test matrix

Document results separately for TV, Android/Fire TV, mobile and desktop. Record connection limits and credential behavior.

Choose your verified credit tier

Begin with a manageable pool. Confirm expiry, renewal, unused-credit, cancellation and sub-reseller rules in writing.

Create clear customer plans

Separate duration from connection count. State compatible devices, support boundaries, refund policy and renewal process.

Activate one controlled customer

Create access in the official vendor panel, deliver credentials privately, verify the first login and document the expiry date.

Track renewals and incidents

Maintain a secure customer register, renewal reminders and an incident log. Never store passwords or sensitive customer data unnecessarily.
